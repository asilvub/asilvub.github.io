Why These Notes Exist

A personal Pensieve for ideas, questions, and thoughts I may want to retrieve later.

August 8, 2026 2 min read

On this page

Purpose What belongs here Status of the notes

I often learn a result, work through a derivation, or follow a line of thought and feel that I will remember it. Often I do not. I think of this collection as my version of Dumbledore’s Pensieve: a place to put ideas so I can retrieve them later.

Purpose

Each entry preserves whatever seems worth retrieving: a mathematical argument, an economic intuition, a question, a method, or a developing line of thought. There is no target length or fixed form. Some entries may settle one small question; others may grow into long explorations.

Working rule An entry is successful if it helps me recover a thought—or continue it—without reconstructing everything from scratch.

What Belongs Here

The collection will mainly cover mathematics, economics, and empirical or computational methods. A typical note might contain:

  • a result stated in plain language;
  • the assumptions that make it true;
  • a compact derivation or proof;
  • an example or counterexample;
  • an unfinished thought or question worth returning to;
  • and links to the sources from which I learned it.

The subject determines the length. A compact observation may need only a few paragraphs, while a difficult idea may require a substantial essay, derivation, or collection of examples.

Status of the Notes

These are public working notes, not publications. I may revise them when I relearn a topic, discover a mistake, or find a clearer explanation. Where accuracy depends on a source, the entry should identify it; where an explanation is my own synthesis, it should say so. Some entries were written and edited in part with OpenAI Codex.

Using these notes

If a note here is useful, please link directly to it and acknowledge it as the source.

Parts of this collection were written and edited with assistance from OpenAI Codex.
